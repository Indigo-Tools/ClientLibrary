Bedezu Client was the first client to have a mod menu in MCBE texture packs, pretty revolutionary back in the day. It was also the first client to remake Lunar Client's UI on Bedrock. Originally, it was made by Bedezu, HJN, and Chixuty. Then Chixuty started making his own client, which was Turtle Client (later renamed to Asteroid Client), but that's not important right now. Bedezu was then continued by Bedezu and HJN. Later on, Lumo and Pepe, a name you'll hear a lot, joined as well. Lumo left pretty quickly, and then HJN left for his own project. So only Bedezu and Pepe remained, and they made the final few versions, which were skidded from Project Light by Pepe. As of 2026, Bedezu is now discontinued and has earned its place in the history of MCBE pack clients, as it deserves.
