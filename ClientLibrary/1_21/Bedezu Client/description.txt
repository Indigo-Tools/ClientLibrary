The Bedezu Client was the first to feature a mod menu in Minecraft Bedrock Edition (MCBE) texture packs, which was pretty revolutionary back in the day. It was also the first client to recreate the UI of Lunar Client on Bedrock. It was originally made by Bedezu, HJN and Chixuty. Then Chixuty started making his own client, Turtle Client (later renamed Asteroid Client), but that's not important right now. Work on Bedezu then continued with Bedezu and HJN. Later on, Lumo and Pepe joined as well — a name you'll hear a lot. Lumo left quickly, then HJN left to work on his own project. Only Bedezu and Pepe remained, and they created the final versions, which were based on Project Light by Pepe. lliT joined the team during the later development of the final versions, but no official release ever came of it, with the latest beta being Bedezu Client 0.0.7. As of 2026, Bedezu has been discontinued, but it has earned its place in the history of MCBE pack clients.
