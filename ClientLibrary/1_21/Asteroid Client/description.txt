Asteroid Client, made by Chixuty, is one of the most famous clients and is usually a top 5 contender in PatarHD's MCBE texture pack client rankings. Asteroid, Bedezu, and CM Client are all based on the same base VDX Java UI by CrisXolt, and Chixuty had a part in two of them (Asteroid and Bedezu). Asteroid is the successor to Turtle Client by Bari. Chixuty is the current owner/developer, along with other collaborators. He also faked his death for no reason, and recently changed his Discord name to Adolf Hitler.
