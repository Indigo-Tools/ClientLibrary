Luconia was first named "Curation Client" by Cxrdion (who is actually Lumo using a different name). Then, Cxrdion created a new Discord account (the Lumo account), gave the ownership of the Curation Discord server to his new account, and rebranded it to Luconia, giving it a fresh start. Luconia Client was one of the most popular clients ever made. It was the first one to release a working mod menu for PCs and mobile devices. A lot of people were upset about it because the client community was at its best. Some of the problems include stolen code and the Discord moderator "Blue Boy". People were really looking forward to Luconia v2, but it never came out. The person who made it, Lumo, stopped working on it and started a new channel on YouTube called "Flxshed." The new owner, Nyami, never really managed to create version 2, but they did create a beta DLL for Luconia. They promised a better version, which of course never happened or is still in the works.
