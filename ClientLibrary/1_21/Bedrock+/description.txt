Chyves' Bedrock+ was one of the first clients to use the oreUI style. Although a full version was never released, we still obtained some beta leaks. At one point during development, Bedrock+ (or Chyves) attempted to collaborate with Better Bedrock in an attempt to optimise its code, although nothing official ever came of it. Towards the end of its lifespan, the texture pack turned into a DLL client for Windows, causing the project to eventually shift into Selaura.
