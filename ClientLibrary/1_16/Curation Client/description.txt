Curation Client was probably the first client ever created by Lumo/Velan, who was still known as Cxrdion at the time. Although it was mainly a buggy combination of multiple packs, it already set the trend for cosmetics. For unknown reasons, he quit the Cxrdion account and created the Lumo/Velan account, going on to create Luconia and Azelux later on.

The screenshots show an old vote held by Cxrdion (account now named ChapterIII, Discord ID: velisa_1) in 2022 regarding the new logo for the client, which includes unused concepts for the start screen.
