As Pepe said, 'Glacier client before glacier'. Project Light was the predecessor to Glacier Client, although it actually came after Glacier Client v3. Back then, Glacier still wasn't popular. There were four versions before it was changed into Glacier Client sometime in 2024. Project Light came about by combining several smaller client creators into one big team consisting of Pepe (who now works on Glacier Client), Flexyus (who is barely active), Kyra (who has left) and mclocked (who now produces phonk music). You can still find relics of that time on the current Glacier Client Discord server.

Fun fact: Papier (the Glacier Discord owner) bought the Discord from Zivflix (the ZF Client owner) for $10.
