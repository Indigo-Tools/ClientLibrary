# Glacier Client

Glacier Client is a clean, highly optimized, and functional utility client and Json UI texture pack for Minecraft Bedrock Edition (MCBE). Designed to enhance your vanilla gameplay experience, it introduces a robust suite of interface modifications and utility overlays tailored for casual play, competitive PvP, and everything in between.

The client is fully compatible across multiple platforms, supporting **Windows**, **Android**, and **iOS**.

## 🚀 Key Features

### 🛠️ Custom Mod Menu & Layouts
* **Modular Interface:** Access a dedicated menu to toggle, manage, and configure in-game utilities.
* **Dynamic Movable Mods:** Complete flexibility to move, scale, and reposition HUD modules anywhere on your screen for a personalized setup.

### 📊 Advanced HUD Components
* **Armor HUD:** Live tracking of equipped armor pieces and current durability.
* **Status HUD:** Clear, compact visualization of active status effects and durations.
* **Direction HUD:** On-screen compass headings to assist with world navigation.
* **In-Game Counters:** Specialized item counters tracking your remaining Totems, Splash Potions, and Arrows.
* **Day-Night Cycle Display:** A precise display showing the progression of the in-game daylight cycle.

### 📍 Waypoints & Tracking
* **Custom Waypoints:** Pin and save specific coordinates to easily navigate back to key areas.
* **Death Waypoints:** Automatically marks your previous death location to streamline inventory recovery.

### 🎛️ Quality-of-Life Enhancements
* **Brewing Stand UI Guide:** Enhanced potion-making layouts that display brewing recipes directly within the interface.
* **Optimized Container Tables:** Extended options and cleaner grid arrangements for standard utility tables.
* **Visual Styling:** Fully customizable crosshairs, unique hotbar themes, and styled XP bars.
