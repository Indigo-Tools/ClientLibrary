Better Bedrock is arguably the most popular texture pack client available. It originally started off as "Utility Pack" by Ambro (as can be seen in the screenshot), but was eventually released as Better Bedrock. The amount of drama that this client has been through is crazy, with the most notable controversy being the first mod menu that could be used on PC. After Ambro and/or TLGM figured out how to do it, the method quickly spread, resulting in a race to see who could release the first client with a mod menu for both PC and mobile. Luconia Client won the race by releasing in a rough state but with a working mod menu. Nonetheless, the client has remained popular over the years, rarely releasing new versions every now and then. These days, Better Bedrock also has a website where you can download other packs and an external PC client. The biggest downside for me is the switch from its original style to oreUI, which makes it look kind of boring now. Also, for some reason, with each patch, more features are removed than added, which I find funny.
