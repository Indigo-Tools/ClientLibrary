Lumo is probably one of the most well-known members of the client community, for better or worse. He is famously known for creating Luconia Client, as well as Curation and Azelux Client. He is also known for not handling criticism well, trying to create an official Lunar Client port for Bedrock, being unable to create a v2 for any of his newer clients, and, of course, always arguing with the creator of Better Bedrock, Ambro. After Curation, he switched accounts (from Cxrdion to Lumo), took a break, and started a fairly successful YouTube channel under the name Flxshed. He would later return to client development in 2025. His comeback was rather successful, and Azelux gathered a large fanbase under his new name, Velan. Recently, he has become inactive again, barely active on Discord and not posting on YouTube.
