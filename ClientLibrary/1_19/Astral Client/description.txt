The first MCBE client of all time as a texture pack. It had a competitor named BadTiger by HJN. The rivalry was great. This was also a client which copied Lunar Client UI. Later on, around v7, they summoned a huge dev team like the Avengers: White, Chainsketch, Pepe, TLGM, Bari, Chyves, etc., with a new unique UI design custom made, not copied from Lunar. It had a mod menu and a few standard mods, but eventually it died as well.
