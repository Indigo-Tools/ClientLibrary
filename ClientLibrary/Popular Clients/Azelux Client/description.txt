 After Luconia, the main developer, Velan, focused on his YouTube channel. After some time, he returned to pack development and created Azelux under his new name, Velan. The Azelux Client is the spiritual successor of the Luconia Client. It is the first client to offer an easy way to save your configuration and cosmetics. While V1 was designed to resemble the Lunar Client, Azelux was originally intended to resemble the Feather Client, as can be seen in the alpha versions. To "perfect" this client, Velan enlisted the help of other developers. lliT is a lead developer and owner, and Pepe helped with several modules. The Azelux codebase is just a mix of the Glacier and Cobalt clients, with some additions. After much anticipation for V1, the release was rather disappointing, as the game crashed on low-end devices. No fix has ever been released for this issue, with Velan saying, "Just wait for v2." As the spiritual successor of Luconia, V2 was never released, with most of the developers quitting after V1. This left only Velan on the team, who is rarely active nowadays, dooming another anticipated release to never see the light of day.
