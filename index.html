Settings

Theme
Choose between Auto (follow system), Dark, Light, or Schedule to switch automatically based on time of day.
Accent color
#
Pick a preset, drop in a custom hex value (e.g. #6c5ce7), or roll the dice for a random accent. Used across buttons, highlights and focus rings.
Night Shift
Applies a warm overlay to reduce blue light at night. Adjust strength below once enabled.
Night Shift strength
Density
Cozy gives content more breathing room; Compact tightens spacing so more cards fit on screen at once.
Compact file cards
Hides the banner art on file cards and tightens padding for a denser list view.
Language
Switches the interface language. Some user-generated client descriptions remain in their original language.
Reduce motion
Disables non-essential animations and transitions. Helpful for motion sensitivity or low-power devices.
Auto-collapse on switch
Automatically collapses expanded client cards when switching between categories, keeping the view tidy.
Reset preferences
Restores theme, accent, density and all other UI preferences to their defaults. Your favorites are not affected.
Your stats
Opens a personalised dashboard with totals, recent activity and favourite counts.
Command palette
A keyboard-driven launcher (Ctrl+K) for jumping to clients, categories and actions instantly.
Export favorites
Saves your favorites list to a JSON file, or restores it on another device. The format is forward-compatible.
